Appointment Guide · Punta Cana

What to Bring to a Nail Appointment in Punta Cana

You do not need a complicated checklist. A few details before the appointment can make it much easier to get the shape, finish and timing you actually want.

Published October 1, 2026 · Nails by Masiel

Bring the design idea, not just the service name

A reference photo can communicate color, shape and detail faster than trying to describe everything from memory. If you do not have a photo, save a few words such as short natural nails, thin French tip, milky pink or simple chrome accent.

If you are unsure whether you need Gel Polish, Rubber Base or Builder Gel, describe the result you want instead of guessing the product.

Know what is already on your nails

Tell Masiel if you currently have gel, structured gel, tips or another product on the nail. Removal can affect how much time the appointment needs.

Also mention a recent break, split or sensitivity before the service begins.

Share the reason if there is a deadline

If the manicure is for a wedding, birthday dinner, photo session or another fixed event, include the event time when you request the appointment.

That makes it easier to avoid an appointment window that is too close to transportation or getting-ready time.

Save the exact Pueblo Bávaro location

Punta Cana covers a wide area. Wait for confirmation, save the map pin and check current travel time from your hotel, apartment or work location shortly before leaving.

Using the exact destination is more reliable than telling a driver only that you are going to Pueblo Bávaro.

Local note: Nails by Masiel has one appointment location on Calle 6ta in Pueblo Bávaro, Punta Cana. Confirm your appointment before traveling.
